AaronClipper

Uses YouTube API Services

AaronClipper is an internal tool run by Aaron Loucks. It posts short vertical clips from Christian sermons and podcasts, shared with the creators' permission, to our own three channels on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat. Every clip credits the original show and links to the full episode.

The tool is not offered to the public, and no one but its owner signs in to it.

Privacy Policy

What AaronClipper does

AaronClipper only publishes videos to accounts we own and operate. It is not offered to other users, and no one else can sign in to it.

YouTube API Services

AaronClipper uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to our own YouTube channels, set their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy, and delete our own uploads. By using AaronClipper, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access, use and store

When we connect our own channels, Google gives AaronClipper an access token that lets it upload and manage videos on those channels. AaronClipper does not access, collect, store or share personal data about viewers or any other YouTube users. It stores only:

  • the access tokens for our own channels,
  • the IDs of videos it uploaded, so they can be deleted on request.

Both are kept only on the computer that runs AaronClipper. They are never sold or shared with third parties, and no cookies or tracking are used.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, or on request, we delete the stored tokens and upload records within 30 days. To request deletion of any data, email aaronlou06@gmail.com.

Other platforms

The same rules apply to the tokens for our TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat accounts. Viewers of our posts are covered by each platform's own privacy policy: TikTok, Meta, Snap.
